\section{Top page format}

At the top of the first page, please include the title and information about all authors, namely their names, affiliations, addresses, and email addresses, must be included. 
Additionally, an abstract of approximately 100 words and a list of keywords must be provided. 
There is no need for the authors to specify the article number.

\section{The number of pages}

Short papers should be no less than 2 pages and about 4 pages in length. 
Long papers should be no less than 4 pages and about 8 pages in length.

\section{Paper style}

Paper must be styled as A4, 9-point font, double-columned, and single spacing. 
Please list the references at the end of the paper~\cite{Codd1970}.
